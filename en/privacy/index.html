Privacy policy

Beltkeep keeps your journal on your phone. There is no account.

What is stored, and where

Everything you enter — classes, notes, techniques, photos, belts, goals, your schedule and settings — is stored in a database and in files on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and we run no server that could receive it.

What is not collected

No name, email address, phone number, location, contacts or health data. No analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Beltkeep does not use HealthKit.

Photos

If you attach a photo to a class, you choose it with the system photo picker, and Beltkeep receives only the photos you pick. They are kept in the app's own storage on your device.

Purchases and RevenueCat

Beltkeep Pro is sold through the App Store, and your device decides whether you have access. To follow subscriptions, the app also tells RevenueCat, a service that handles in-app purchases, about your purchases. RevenueCat receives your purchase receipt and transaction details from Apple, an anonymous app user ID that it generates, and the technical data that comes with any network request, such as your IP address, device model, system version, language and country. It does not receive your journal or anything else you enter. We cannot tell who you are from this, and RevenueCat does not use it to track you across other companies' apps or websites. RevenueCat's own policy, at revenuecat.com/privacy, describes how it handles that data.

Network access

Apart from the purchase processing described above, and Apple's own App Store traffic when you buy or restore, the app makes no network requests.

Notifications

Reminders and the round timer's alerts are local notifications created on your phone. There is no push server, and nothing about them is sent anywhere.

Backups and exports: what leaves the device, and only when you send it

If you choose a backup folder, for example on iCloud Drive, Beltkeep copies your journal and your photos there after every class. That folder is yours; what is stored in it is kept by the service you chose, under that service's policy. A JSON or CSV export leaves the device only when you choose to share or save it, and goes only where you send it.

Children

Beltkeep collects no personal data from anyone, children included. It is a training journal and is not directed at children.

Deleting your data

A deleted class waits in Recently deleted for 30 days and is then removed. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your phone, permanently, so keep a backup or an export if you want to keep your journal. Copies in a backup folder are yours to delete in Files. Purchase records held by Apple and RevenueCat follow their own policies.
