Common questions

The journal will not open

Beltkeep shows a recovery screen instead of an empty journal, and nothing is deleted. Tap Try again first. If it still fails, choose Restore from backup folder and pick your backup folder or file, or Export raw files and send them to us with Contact support. The old files move to a folder called Recovered; they are never erased.

How do I back up my journal?

Settings → Backup and export → Choose folder. Pick a folder on iCloud Drive or in Files, and Beltkeep copies your journal there after every class, photos included. You can also export JSON or CSV at any time.

How do I move my journal to a new iPhone?

Set up the backup folder on the old phone. On the new one, tap I have a backup when you start and choose the same folder. Your iPhone backup carries the journal across as well.

I deleted a class by mistake

Settings → Recently deleted. A deleted class waits there for 30 days, and you can restore it.

How is the streak counted?

It counts weeks in a row in which you reached your weekly goal. A class counts when it lasts at least 20 minutes and is not conditioning. A week you pause neither counts nor breaks it.

Why did my reminders stop?

Beltkeep sends at most two reminders a day and stays quiet at night. Check that notifications are allowed for Beltkeep in your iPhone's Settings, and the switches in Beltkeep's Settings → Reminders.

What happens when my trial or subscription ends?

Nothing is deleted: your journal stays on your phone and Export my data stays available. Beltkeep asks you to choose a plan before you can add or open classes again, and when you do, everything is as you left it.

I subscribed, but the app still asks me to choose a plan

Tap Restore purchase on the plan screen, signed in to the Apple Account you bought with. Restoring is free and brings back any plan that is still active.

How do I cancel the subscription?

In Beltkeep: Settings → Plan and billing → Manage subscription, or on your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the free trial or the paid period ends. You keep access until the end of the period you have paid for.

Is there a widget or an Apple Watch app?

Not in this version. Beltkeep does add two actions for Siri and Shortcuts: Log a class and Open the round timer.
