Terms of use

The short version: Beltkeep is a journal. It is not coaching, not medical advice, and not a promise about how you will train or compete.

What Beltkeep is

Beltkeep keeps a record of your Brazilian jiu-jitsu training: the classes you log, your notes and techniques, your belts, your goals and the charts that follow from them. It shows what you entered; it does not judge it.

No coaching, no medical advice

Beltkeep is not a coach, a doctor or a medical device. Martial arts carry a risk of injury: train within your limits, listen to your body and your coach, and talk to a professional if you are unsure. Numbers and charts are a record of what you entered, not advice or a promise.

An independent app

Beltkeep is not affiliated with or endorsed by any federation, brand or academy. Belt names and technique names are the common words of the sport.

Purchases

Beltkeep has no free tier. Both plans, yearly and monthly, start with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers; if you do not cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, your Apple Account is charged for the first period. The subscription then renews automatically every year or month until you cancel it in your Apple Account settings, and you keep access until the end of the period you have paid for. If a plan ends, nothing is deleted and you can export your journal. Payments are processed by Apple; RevenueCat is told about purchases, as the privacy policy describes. Refunds are handled by Apple under its own rules. Use Restore purchase to get your plan back on another device with the same Apple Account. Apple's standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement applies to the app.

Your data

Your records are stored on your device and are yours. There is no server copy, which also means a lost phone without a backup is lost data. Set up the backup folder in Settings and keep it.
