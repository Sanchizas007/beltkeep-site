What it does

  • Logs in 20 seconds. Type, length, rolls, subs, taps, how hard it felt and a note: six touches or fewer.
  • Keeps everything. Every class by month, as a list or a calendar, with photos and a focus for your next class.
  • Knows your game. A library of 160+ techniques, Gi and No-Gi. Mark what you are learning, count what lands and what catches you, and add your own.
  • Shows your progress. Mat time, a year heat map, subs and taps per ten rolls, belts and stripes, a weekly goal and a streak.
  • Times your rounds. Round, rest and warm-up bells that sound with the screen off.
  • Never loses a roll. The journal lives on your phone and is copied to a folder you choose after every class. Export it as JSON or CSV at any time.

What it does not do

Beltkeep has no account, no social feed and no ads, and it does not use HealthKit. It is an independent app, not affiliated with or endorsed by any federation, brand or academy. It is a journal, not medical advice: train within your limits and listen to your body and your coach.
